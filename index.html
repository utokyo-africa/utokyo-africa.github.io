About

An alumni association linking UTokyo and Africa

UTokyo alumni from Africa are spread across the continent, but an organized network has not yet been fully developed. Africa Akamonkai aims to connect alumni, current students, and early-career researchers as one community spanning the whole continent, rather than country by country.

Mission

What we aim to do

Communication

Promote mutual communication and support among UTokyo alumni across Africa.

Student Support

Support current students and early-career researchers.

Bridge Building

Serve as a bridge between Japan and Africa in academic, business, and social fields.

Visibility

Increase the visibility of UTokyo research and education activities in Africa.

Innovation

Foster innovation and entrepreneurship within the alumni community.

Activities

Main activity areas

Networking and Community Building

Alumni database, regular exchanges, and online information sharing.

Student and Researcher Support

Support for current students, mentoring, and scholarship or research opportunity sharing.

Japan-Africa Collaboration

Research collaboration, business matching, entrepreneurship, and visiting researcher support.

Public Relations and Visibility

Annual reports, newsletters, and social media communication.
